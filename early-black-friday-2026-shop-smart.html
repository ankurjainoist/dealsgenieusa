🛒 Early Black Friday 2026: A Simple Plan to Shop Smart

Black Friday 2026 falls on November 27, and many stores start their sales well before that. Amazon's own October event, Prime Big Deal Days, ran October 6-7. If you missed it, you have time to plan for the next round without rushing.

Step 1: Write a short list

List the five or six things you actually plan to buy or gift. For each one, write the maximum you are willing to pay. A list protects you from buying something only because it has a big discount tag.

Step 2: Know the normal price

A discount only means something when you compare it with what the item usually costs. Look at how the price has moved over recent weeks using the price history that some tools show, and be careful with 'was' prices you cannot verify.

Step 3: Check the seller and the return window

Prefer items sold and shipped by Amazon or a seller with a strong rating, and read the return policy. For gifts, compare the return window with the date you plan to give them.

Step 4: Compare similar products

The first search result is not always the best value. Open two or three alternatives and compare ratings, the number of reviews and what is included in the box. The middle-star reviews usually describe real trade-offs.

Step 5: Use coupons and promo codes carefully

Some listings have a coupon you need to clip, and some deals use a promo code at checkout. Both can end at any time, so check that the discount is applied before you place the order.

Step 6: Watch delivery dates

For holiday gifts the delivery estimate matters as much as the price. If an item will not arrive in time, it is not a good deal.

Step 7: Avoid impulse buying

If something is not on your list, wait a day. Good offers come back around often.
