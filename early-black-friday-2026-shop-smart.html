🛒 Early Black Friday 2026: How to Shop Smart (and Not Get Fooled)

Oct 13, 2026 • DealsGenie

Black Friday deals often start weeks before the actual day. That is good news if you plan ahead, and bad news if you shop on impulse.

Step 1: Make a short list

Write down 5 things you really need or plan to gift. A list stops random buying.

Step 2: Set a budget for each item

Decide the maximum you will pay. If the deal price is above it, skip.

Step 3: Learn the normal price

A product marked 50% off is not special if its normal price was already low. Check price history for the last 60-90 days.

Step 4: Check the seller and return policy

Prefer items sold by Amazon or a trusted seller, and check the return window before you buy.

Step 5: Do not wait for the last minute

Popular items sell out. If a deal matches your list and budget, buying early can be the smart move.
