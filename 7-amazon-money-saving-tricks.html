🤫 7 Amazon Money-Saving Tricks Most People Don't Know

Most shoppers search, click and pay. A few simple habits can lower your total without much extra effort. Here are seven worth trying, with a note on how to use each one.

1. Look for the coupon box

Many listings show a coupon box under the price. It is not applied automatically, so you have to tick or clip it before checkout. It is easy to miss, so look for it every time.

2. Check Amazon Resale

Amazon Resale sells returned and open-box items with the condition clearly graded. Read the grade and the description first, and check the return policy.

3. Try Subscribe & Save for things you reorder

For items you buy regularly, such as coffee, detergent or toothpaste, a subscription can lower the price. You can usually cancel or skip a delivery, so set a reminder so you only pay for what you use.

4. Check how the price has moved

A discount is only real if the old price was real. Some tools and listings show how a price changed over time. If the price was higher only for a few days before the sale, be careful.

5. Put items in your cart and wait

If an item is not in short supply, leave it in your cart or on a list for a day or two. Sometimes the price changes. Do not wait on limited deals that may sell out.

6. Compare similar products

The second or third result is often similar in quality. Compare ratings, the number of reviews and what is included, and read the 3-star reviews for honest trade-offs.

7. Check the delivery and the return window

A lower price does not help if the item arrives late or is hard to return. Check both before you pay.

Offers and prices on Amazon change, so always check the current details on the product page.
