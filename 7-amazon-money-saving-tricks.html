🤫 7 Amazon Money-Saving Tricks Most People Don't Know

Oct 13, 2026 • DealsGenie

Most shoppers just search, click and pay. A few simple habits can cut your bill without any extra effort. Here are 7 worth trying.

1. Look for the coupon box

Many products have a clip-on coupon right under the price. It is easy to miss, and it is not applied automatically.

2. Check Amazon Resale

Open-box and returned items are often sold at a discount with the condition clearly listed. Read the grade before buying.

3. Try Subscribe & Save for things you reorder

Items like detergent, coffee or toothpaste can be cheaper when you subscribe. You can cancel any time, so set a reminder.

4. Track the price before you buy

Free price-history tools show if a deal is really a deal. If the price was higher only for a few days before the sale, skip it.

5. Put items in your cart and wait

Sometimes the price drops after a day or two. Do not rush unless the deal is limited.

6. Compare similar products

The second or third result often has similar quality at a lower price. Read the 3-star reviews to find out.

7. Use a price-drop alert

Save the item to a list and watch for drops instead of buying at the first price you see.
