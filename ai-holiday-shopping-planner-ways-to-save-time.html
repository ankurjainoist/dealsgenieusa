🎁 5 Ways to Use AI to Plan Your Holiday Shopping

The holiday rush is easier when you plan early, and an AI chatbot can help with the boring parts. Here are five practical ways to use one, none of which need special skills.

1. Turn your list into a plan

Paste a rough list of people and ideas and ask the chatbot to organize it by person, category or deadline. You can also ask it to flag which gifts need shipping time so you order them first.

2. Brainstorm gifts for specific people

Describe the person's age, interests and what they already own, and ask for ten ideas in different styles: practical, fun and sentimental. Use the ideas as a starting point, then check what is actually available and suitable.

3. Compare two products you are considering

Ask for a side-by-side comparison of the features that matter to you. Keep in mind that the chatbot may not know the latest versions, so confirm the details on the product page.

4. Draft a checklist for the big days

Black Friday is on November 27, 2026. Ask the chatbot to create a checklist: what to buy, your maximum for each item, where to check delivery dates and when to stop shopping. A checklist cuts impulse buys.

5. Write thoughtful notes quickly

Use AI to draft a short, personal gift note or message, then edit it so it sounds like you.

A few cautions

AI can make mistakes, so verify prices, availability and delivery dates yourself. Do not share private information such as payment details. And remember that a good deal still needs to fit your budget and your list.
