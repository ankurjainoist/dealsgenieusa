🔍 How to Spot a Fake Amazon Deal

Oct 11, 2026 • DealsGenie

Not every discount is a real discount. Do these three checks before buying.

1. Check the price history

Use a price tracker to see if the old price was real or inflated just before the sale.

2. Read the 3-star reviews

They are usually the most honest. 5-star can be fake and 1-star can be angry.

3. Check who is selling

Prefer items sold and shipped by Amazon or a seller with a high rating.
