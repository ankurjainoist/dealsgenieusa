✅ Can You Trust AI Product Recommendations? A Quick Checklist

Oct 16, 2026 • DealsGenie

AI product recommendations are everywhere now, from shopping apps to general chatbots. They can be helpful, but they are not a final answer. Use this checklist to decide when to trust a suggestion and when to double-check.

1. Ask why it recommends the product

A good answer explains the reasons: features, use case, size, materials. If the response is vague or only says a product is popular, ask for specifics.

2. Check the product page yourself

Confirm the title, the version or model, the size and compatibility, the seller, the delivery date and the return window. AI answers can mix up similar products.

3. Read real reviews

Look at recent reviews, and read the 3-star ones, which often describe honest trade-offs. Look for repeated comments about the same problem.

4. Compare at least one alternative

Ask for a second option that fits the same need. Comparing two products makes it easier to see what you are paying for.

5. Watch for outdated information

Products are updated often. A chatbot may describe an older model or an offer that has ended. Treat anything about price, coupons or availability as something to verify.

6. Keep your budget in charge

AI will happily suggest more features than you need. Decide your maximum before you ask, and stick to it.

7. Protect your privacy

Do not share payment details, passwords or sensitive personal information in a chat.

AI is a good starting point for research. The final decision still comes from checking the details yourself.
